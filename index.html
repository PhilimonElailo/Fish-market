Protecting Longech Beach, empowering communities, and sustaining Lake Turkana.

Our Mission

To protect the lakefront, restore our shoreline, and support resilient coastal livelihoods.

Longech BMU promotes environmental stewardship through cleanup campaigns, local awareness on water-level change and climate impacts, and sustainable fishing practices that secure food security and community wellbeing.

What We Do

We coordinate community action, member engagement, and responsible fisheries management.

Leadership & Authorities

Guided by BMU executives, committee leaders, and active local stakeholders.

Longech BMU brings together executive officers, committee members, and community leaders to coordinate beach operations, monitor compliance, and lead local environmental action across the shoreline and fishing community.

  • Community sensitization and public campaigns
  • Resource support and sustainable fishing guidance
